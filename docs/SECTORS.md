# Sectors, businesses and the multi-business headquarters

AI Empire HQ runs several independent businesses, grouped into sectors.
Every room and every employee in the virtual offices comes from a real
database record.

## Initial structure

`npm run db:setup` seeds it, and `npm run org:provision` adds it to an existing
database. Both are idempotent and keep existing records.

| Sector | Businesses |
|---|---|
| Nightlife & Entertainment | **XYZ PARTYS**, **305 Events**, **Party Street**, **713 Society** (four separate records) |
| E-Commerce | none yet |
| Investments & Acquisitions | none yet |
| Business Development | Test Business A (development test data) |
| Technology & Automation | none yet |
| Shared Services | Test Business B (development test data) |

- Each sector gets one manager agent (`<Sector> Manager`, role `sector-manager`).
- New businesses start in **planning** status.
- New agents get only `task.read`, `task.execute`, `approval.request` and
  `knowledge.read`, and **no tasks**.
- No paid work ever starts on its own: a person must assign it, and budgets
  still apply.

## Levels and who can do what (`src/backend/org/rules.ts`)

| Level | Agents | Works on | Reads |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headquarters | Chief AI Manager, Central Project Manager | HQ tasks. The **Central PM** may also take tasks in any business, to coordinate. | HQ records. The Chief reads organization-wide records through its read-only tools (`org.read`). |
| Sector | sector manager, sector specialists | Tasks of any business **in its own sector** | That sector's businesses (records and knowledge) |
| Business | business lead, specialists | Tasks of **its own business only** | Its own business only |

**Businesses stay distinct:**
- Business names are unique in an organization, ignoring case. The database enforces this.
- A business agent never works on, or reads, a sibling business, even within the same sector.
- Every business has its own budget and its own knowledge folder.

**Archived businesses take no new work.** No tasks can be created for them, and
no agents can be assigned. A business can only be archived once its open tasks
are finished or canceled. Restoring it returns it to planning.

## Cross-sector project rules

| Project scope | Tasks it accepts | Who may lead it |
|---|---|---|
| Business project | Tasks of that business | Agents allowed to work on that business |
| Sector project | Tasks of any business in that sector | That sector's agents, or headquarters |
| Headquarters project | Tasks of any business in any sector (**cross-sector**) | Headquarters agents |

The task engine enforces assignment rules for every path: the API, the
dashboard, workers and the Chief's work requests. Project rules are enforced
when a task is created.

## Who can change the structure

These actions require a **person** with the `businesses:admin` scope and
organization-wide access. Agent tokens never get this scope.

- add a business
- archive or restore a business
- change a business's status (planning / active / paused)
- create a sector manager, business lead or specialist

Creating a project needs `tasks:write` and access to the project's scope.
Every change is written to the audit log.

## Budgets

- Organization and business budgets work as before: "no budget" means no
  spending.
- A **sector budget** is optional. If a sector has one for the month, it must
  have room too.

```bash
npm run budget:set -- --usd 2 --sector nightlife-entertainment
```

## Reporting and offices

- `/sectors` lists every sector with its businesses, agents, open tasks, API
  spend for the month, its budget and its manager.
- `/sectors/<slug>` is the sector report:
  - businesses with status, agents, tasks, spend and budget
  - the sector team, the business teams, and headquarters oversight
  - projects, including cross-sector projects with tasks in this sector
  - administration forms
- Reporting applies business-level access. A token limited to one business
  sees only that business. Sector-wide figures (sector team, sector projects,
  sector budget) need access to the whole sector.
- `/sectors/<slug>/office` is the sector's office (PixiJS). It's generated by
  `buildSectorFloor` from the sector's non-archived businesses:
  - one sector office for the sector team, and one room per business
  - each room seats up to four of its own agents; further agents are listed
    as "not on this floor"
  - characters move only on real execution events (see
    [LIVE_OFFICE.md](LIVE_OFFICE.md))
  - the elevator returns to the Executive Floor, and the Executive Floor's
    elevator opens `/sectors`
